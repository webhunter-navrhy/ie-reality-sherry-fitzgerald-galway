Set amidst approx. 0.64 acres of beautifully manicured gardens, Pine Hurst is an exceptional detached residence offering privacy, space, and tranquillity just 800m from the heart of Barna village. This impressive five-bedroom home combines the charm of a peaceful countryside setting with the convenience of one of Galway's most sought-after coastal locations.Built in the 1981 and meticulously maintained and upgraded over the years, Pine Hurst extends to a generous single-storey layout designed for comfortable family living. A welcoming entrance hall leads to a superb split-level sitting room with direct access to the patio and gardens, while a spacious living room on the opposite side of the home provides an ideal space for relaxation and entertaining. The property features five well-proportioned bedrooms, three of which benefit from en suite facilities, offering excellent accommodation. At the heart of the home is a large open-plan kitchen and dining area, flooded with natural light and enjoying panoramic sea views. The property enjoys fine views over Barna Village onto Galway Bay. The extensive grounds are a standout feature of this impressive home, with mature landscaping, expansive lawns, and a wonderful sense of privacy. Complementing the main residence are a large insulated garage, A self-contained detached studio ideal as home office, gym or a range of uses with timber floor, kitchen unit, shower room and has independent access. There is also a substantial storage unit. Barna is one of Galway's most desirable seaside villages overlooking the stunning waters of Galway Bay. Galway City is less than 8 km away, ensuring easy access to all the cultural, educational and commercial amenities the city has to offer.Pine Hurst represents a rare opportunity to acquire a distinguished family home in an outstanding location where lifestyle, convenience and coastal living come together seamlessly, together with additional accommodation. Garden:Natural stone boundary walls and a pillared entrance open onto a gravel driveway, providing ample parking to the front, side, and rear of the property. The extensive mature gardens are complemented by a variety of trees, shrub beds, and established laurel hedging. Raised patio areas to the front and side of the residence feature natural stone paving, creating attractive outdoor spaces. The detached garage benefits from double vehicular access doors and is fully insulated, with electrical connections, two Velux roof windows, and generous storage space. A section of the garage is currently utilised as a utility area and is plumbed for a washing machine and dryer.The studio features engineered timber flooring, fitted kitchen units, a Belfast sink, plumbing for a washing machine, and independent side access. The open-plan living/sleeping area enjoys excellent natural light through sliding patio doors and includes access to a dressing area with carpet flooring and fitted shelving. Completing the accommodation is a shower room with tiled flooring, WC, wash hand basin, and shower unit.
